PUT YOUR MUSIC FILE IN THIS FOLDER
==================================
Supported: .wav  .mp3  .ogg
  - .wav plays out of the box on Windows (no extra install).
  - .mp3 / .ogg need pygame:   pip install pygame

Your files automatically replace the built-in theme (_default_theme.wav).
In-game:  M = music on/off     N = next track
If you use the .exe, keep this "music" folder in the SAME folder as the .exe.
